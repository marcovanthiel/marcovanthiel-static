# Accessibility statement

The website marcovanthiel.nl aims to be usable for everyone. This site therefore strives for accessibility according to WCAG 2.2, level AA. This is in line with the European Accessibility Act and the EN 301 549 standard.

## Measures

- Accessibility is part of the design of this site.
- The site is checked periodically with an automated scan, including the in-house service [ToegankelijkScan](/toegankelijkscan/) at marcovanthiel.nl/toegankelijkscan.

## Status

To the best of our knowledge, this site complies fully or largely with WCAG 2.2, level AA. No deviations are known at this time.

## Report a problem

Do you encounter an accessibility problem on this site? Please report it via [marco@marcovanthiel.nl](mailto:marco@marcovanthiel.nl). You will receive a response as soon as possible.

This statement was drawn up on 10 September 2026.
